# Rewriting a flagged line

Read this when a rewrite is not coming out right, or for DMs and comments.

## The order of priorities

1. **Meaning and facts stay.** Numbers, names, product names, prices, dates, the call-to-action keyword ("Comment GUIDE") and any promise made to the viewer come through unchanged. If cutting a stock phrase leaves a line with nothing in it, the line probably was filler: cut it rather than invent content.
2. **It sounds like them.** Their range of sentence length, their contractions, their person, their punctuation, the way they end. Their repeated phrases are a resource, not a quota.
3. **It passes the check.** Last, not first. A line that is exactly what they would say and still trips a rule stays; say why in the report.

## Turning shapes into plain sentences

| Flagged shape | Usually becomes |
|---|---|
| "It's not just a tool, it's a teammate." | What it does that a teammate does: "It does the follow-ups for you." |
| "The result? Two hours back every week." | "I got two hours back every week." |
| "Here's the truth: most people quit." | "Most people quit." |
| "No setup. No code. Just results." | One line saying what it is: "It works without setup or code." |
| "Whether you're a founder or a freelancer, ..." | Name the one person the post is for, or drop the clause. |
| "In today's fast-paced world, ..." | Start at the second half of the sentence. |
| "Ready to take your X to the next level?" | Their own usual ending, usually the call to action on its own. |
| "We utilize ... to ensure ..." | "I use ... so ..." (their person, everyday verbs). |

These are directions, not templates. Do not swap one stock shape for another.

## Scripts

A script is heard once at talking speed. Keep each line to one idea, put the subject early, and avoid anything that only works on the page: brackets, slashes, "the former", long lists. Say every new line in your head; if it needs rehearsing, it is still written, not spoken.

## Captions

The first line shows before "more", so it carries the point. Keep their usual length and hashtag habit. If they never use emoji, a rewrite has none.

## DMs and comments

Short, direct, in reply to someone. Length rules do not apply. Match how they talk to people, which is often looser than their captions: if the profile was built only from captions, say so when a DM rewrite feels too formal. Never add a sales line to a reply that did not have one.

## When to stop

Two rounds of check and rewrite. If something is still flagged after that, show it with the reason you kept it. A creator would rather fix one line themselves than read a draft that has been sanded flat.
